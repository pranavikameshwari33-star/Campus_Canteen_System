Meal timings

The customer menu changes automatically with the time (India time). Customers can order only while a session is open and the canteen is open (07:30 - 18:00, set in Settings). A session opens at its start time and closes exactly at its end time, when the next one can begin. Active sessions must not overlap. Chinese food can only be sold in Lunch and Snacks sessions.
