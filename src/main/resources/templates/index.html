College Canteen - pre-order your meal

Order and pay before the break, then collect your food with a token instead of waiting in the queue. Open to students and faculty.

Log in Register

How it works

  1. Register with your college roll number or faculty ID, then log in.
  2. Choose items from the menu of the current meal session and add them to your cart.
  3. At checkout, choose Cash (pay at the counter) or QR Code (pay by UPI and send a screenshot).
  4. As soon as you choose, your order joins the queue and you receive a pickup token.
  5. When your order shows Ready for pickup, show your token at the counter to collect it.

Orders are served strictly in the order they were placed. Students and faculty have equal priority.
